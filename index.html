About Me

I'm a Computer Software Application trainee who loves turning ideas into working software. My journey began with the fundamentals of computers, office tools and accounting software, and grew into programming and web development with Python, JavaScript, HTML, CSS and MySQL.

I'm especially interested in software development and Artificial Intelligence. My flagship project, the AI Smart Office Assistant, combines both: it aims to make everyday office work like documents, resumes, spreadsheets and reminders faster and easier.

Because I understand how offices really work, from Excel and Tally to Photoshop and CorelDRAW, I like building tools that real people can use on day one.

Career Goal

To grow into a skilled software developer who builds reliable, user-friendly and AI-powered applications, starting with an internship or entry-level role where I can learn from a great team and contribute from day one.
